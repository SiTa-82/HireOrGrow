Calculation breakdown

Comparison rule: Calculates the change in total costs versus the baseline where existing employees stay in their previous positions and the target position remains unfilled. An external hire adds a full position cost. An internal move counts new payroll − previous payroll + required backfill after the transition, plus direct expenses and estimated losses. Set backfill to zero if the former position will not be replaced, and assess the operational impact separately. This is not a skill assessment or an automated HR recommendation.

Four steps from question to an informed discussion.

1

2

02

Three Different Decision Models

The figures in these charts answer different questions and must not be compared against each other.

MODEL A · ONE-OFF

External vs. Internal Hire

Estimates the one-time cost of filling a vacancy: recruiting/assessment, onboarding, vacancy and ramp-up productivity loss, and any relevant backfill. Ongoing salaries are not part of the main chart. A separate salary-inclusive 12-month projection appears in the detail view when applicable.

MODEL B · TOTAL

Retain vs. Replace

Estimates total modeled costs across the selected time horizon. Retention includes salary, raise and training; replacement includes salary after vacancy, exit/recruiting/onboarding and estimated productivity loss. Results do not justify employee termination.

MODEL C · INCREMENTAL

Hire, Promote, or Reskill

Compares incremental costs relative to keeping existing employees in their previous jobs with the new role unfilled. It accounts for payroll difference and backfill, direct costs and modeled losses. Candidate feasibility and full-productivity time can exclude an internal route from the ranking.

Important: Each chart has its own cost basis and scale. “Lowest cost” means lowest modeled cost among assessed feasible alternatives within that chart, not the best overall staffing choice.

Monthly ↔ Annual

Salary rates change period by multiplying or dividing by 12. This does not convert NET to GROSS. The training budget is a one-off amount and does not change with the switch.

Currency conversion

Published salary evidence uses FX rates when available. The other calculator inputs are not automatically converted when location changes; local taxes are not modeled.

Source hierarchy

Exact role and grade are preferred. Same-role evidence with a different or broader grade may be used as an explicitly labeled indicative estimate. Broader job-family and CTC records remain context only.

Manual internal rate

The initially mirrored market rate is only a proxy, not an organization-verified internal salary. Update it with an appropriate planning figure; reported pay basis is never silently treated as GROSS.

Candidate Feasibility & Readiness

An unavailable option is not a free option.

Candidate Identified

Not Assessed

No Suitable Candidate

Not compared. The chart shows why the internal path is unavailable.

Readiness time is shown separately. Optional monthly business impact can monetize delay in the Hire / Promote / Reskill model, replacing overlapping target-role vacancy and ramp-up losses. Avoid double-counting losses elsewhere.

05

Rate Trends

Observed changes in published sources, not invented historical salaries.

Rate Trends uses dated snapshots saved in the repository. A trend begins only after observations have actually been collected: source publication dates and collection dates are different. Amounts are compared only within the same source, country, role, grade, currency and pay basis.

One snapshot alone cannot establish an upward or downward trend. Missing source updates and market coverage limitations should remain visible.

06

Interpretation, Limits & Privacy

Good decisions require review beyond the displayed totals.

Not an HR recommendation

Calculations do not assess performance, skills, ethics, team capacity, legal risks or retention likelihood. Validate with HR and Finance.

Basis matters

NET, GROSS, unspecified, billing and CTC figures are not equivalent. Employer overhead is an optional user assumption, not a jurisdiction-specific tax model.

Illustrative defaults

Training and promotion-raise defaults draw on US studies, not local quotes. Recruitment and productivity-loss assumptions are manually modeled.

Use fictional data

There is no corporate authentication, enterprise storage, encryption at rest or NDA clearance. Manual inputs stay in browser page memory, but copied/printed reports may expose them.

Public salary sources are shown with their observation dates and links in Salary Benchmarks. Automatic collection depends on source permission and available APIs; unavailable data are not represented as live.
